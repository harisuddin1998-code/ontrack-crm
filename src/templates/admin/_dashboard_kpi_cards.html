{# Administrator KPI card deck. Included by BOTH admin/dashboard.html and admin/executive_dashboard.html so the two pages cannot drift apart in either their numbers or their colours - the Executive dashboard is required to be a replica of the Administrator's. The numbers come from one backend helper (`_compute_admin_dashboard_stats`) and are formatted by one more (`dashboard_sync_values`); this file is the matching front end. Caller passes `sync` - the map of already-formatted display values: {% raw %}{% include 'admin/_dashboard_kpi_cards.html' %}{% endraw %} Every live figure is printed as `{% raw %}{{ sync. }}{% endraw %}` inside an element tagged `data-sync=""`. That tag is what the 120-second refresh writes into (see _live_sync.html), so the card is refreshed by the same map that rendered it and no figure is ever formatted twice. Cards are grouped by the work they belong to, each group under its own heading. Every card is a link, and every link lands on the records that card counted - a number you cannot open is a dead end, and one that opens the wrong list is worse. Colours are the pastel `o_stat_*` tones from odoo_style.css - never a saturated gradient. Every figure here is counted over the timeframe chip at the top of the board, the same window the period sections below use, so the whole page answers one question about one period. That is also why each link carries `since=` - the card's number and the list it opens are the same set. #} {# Sales and purchase orders are one group: a sales order IS a purchase order here, so splitting them into two headings had the same records counted under two names in two places on the same screen. The three status cards split the orders the first card counts, so they add up to it rather than repeating it. #}

{# AMC invoicing. Its own group rather than another card under Revenue & Recovery: that group answers "what came in", and these answer "what was asked for".
